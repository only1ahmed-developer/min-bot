WhatsApp Bot

Enter your number with country code, no leading zero. You need a website account first (create one from the Telegram bot with /createlogins, or from WhatsApp privately with .createlogins), then log in to request a pairing code.

Log in to pair a number

QR pairing is available after login

The dashboard renders a live QR code from the backend once you are signed in.

Telegram Bot

The Telegram bot handles account creation, WhatsApp pairing by command, and group management on Telegram's side.

  • Send /start to see available commands
  • Send /createlogins <username> to get your website login
  • Send /pair <number> to connect a WhatsApp number
Open Telegram Bot

You must join the required group/channels before the bot will respond.
